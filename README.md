# Northstar paper trading

Open `index.html` in a modern browser, or run `python3 -m http.server 8000` in this directory and visit http://localhost:8000. No build step or API keys are required. Fonts fall back to system fonts offline.

Features: 22 synthetic stocks and ETFs (including NVDA, MU, and AVGO), continuous price movements, searchable watchlist, bid/ask spreads, persisted positions, history CSV export, configurable market scenarios, a replayable account timeline, and 16 learning missions.

- Direct call/put ticket with expiration and strike selectors, calls-only/puts-only/combined chains, delta, gamma, theta, vega, implied volatility, intrinsic/time value, and expiration breakeven.
- Candlesticks by default: 1/5/15/60-minute OHLC aggregation, volume, SMA 20, EMA 9, Bollinger 20, RSI 14, crosshair readout, wheel zoom, and an expanded chart view. Indicators operate on the selected candle interval and displayed window.
- Market, limit, stop, stop-limit, trailing sell stop, GTC, and IOC orders. Single-leg trades support partial fills. Protect an owned position with paired stop-loss/take-profit OCO exits. The first fill cancels its partner; any unfilled remainder stays in the triggered order. Canceling an exit manually cancels only that exit.
- Executable strategy builder: bull call, bear put, bull put, bear call, straddle, strangle, iron condor, butterfly, covered call/buy-write, protective put, cash-secured put, and collar templates. Edit one to four same-expiration option legs with ratios of one or two. Stock strategies purchase a dedicated 100-share stock leg per unit inside the package, rather than reusing existing standalone shares. Inspect expiration payoff and maximum risk, adjust price/time/volatility in what-if analysis, then submit one atomic paper order. Strategy orders use market bid/ask fills, not partial or limit fills, and are capped at 50 units.
- Strategy positions with all-leg closing, cash collateral, portfolio Greeks, unrealized gains, and simulated expiration settlement.
- Time machine: advance 1/7/30/90 days, enter a custom jump up to 366 days, or jump exactly to the nearest held option/package expiration. Every jump automatically saves a checkpoint. Save manual checkpoints and restore them, including cash, positions, pending orders, price history, and the PRNG state. Restoration also saves the current state so it can be recovered from the checkpoint selector. The quick rewind button never restores a future-dated checkpoint.
- AI boom, AI expectations reset, broad bull/bear, balanced, and volatile scenarios. Prices use annualized volatility and time-scaled drift, common market and AI-sector shocks, plus synthetic quarterly earnings events (macro catalysts for ETFs). Earnings uncertainty affects implied volatility and event completion reduces it. The algorithm permits gains and losses even in the bullish AI scenario. No live prices, forecasts, or guaranteed profits.
- Northstar Academy: 16 missions covering long/short calls and puts, covered calls, protective puts, cash-secured puts, four vertical spreads, long/short straddles, strangles, collars, and iron condors. Each mission includes a separate $100,000 training account, exact example premiums, three final-price paths, time controls, ATM inspection, optional IV crush, intrinsic/extrinsic values, expiration payoff, physical exercise/assignment illustrations, three quizzes, and completion objectives. Earn 150 XP once per mission (2,400 total), with saved progress and rank badges. Main account funds are unaffected.

The market runs while the page is visible, with one simulated minute per two real seconds at 1× speed. It never closes. 3×/8× acceleration and pause are available. Fast-forward and entering Academy pause the live clock; use Play to resume. Fast-forward processes hourly steps, splitting at expiration and catalyst boundaries. Pending orders execute against these step quotes; intrahour limit/stop touches are not reconstructed. Jumping to expiry uses the exact expiration timestamp. The same checkpoint, scenario, duration, and step schedule reproduce the same market path. Order fills use a separate random stream, so placing a trade does not itself change market noise. Different expiration boundaries or step schedules can still produce different paths.

Active account data is saved to localStorage every six seconds, after account actions, and when leaving the page. Historical bars use a compact array encoding on disk and are decoded on load. Academy progress has a separate localStorage key and is not rolled back by account rewind or account reset. The latest 16 full account checkpoints persist in IndexedDB, avoiding localStorage's small quota. If checkpoint storage is unavailable, checkpoints remain in memory for the current page session and the UI reports the limitation. Use one tab per account; concurrent tabs do not synchronize. Changing browser or origin uses separate storage. Reset clears the active simulator account and starts a new checkpoint session, but does not erase Academy progress.

All prices, order sizes, and volume are synthetic. No real orders or market data. Standalone orders support long stocks/options and selling owned positions. Short options are available only inside fully collateralized multi-leg packages; uncovered call exposure is rejected. The builder holds the maximum intrinsic obligation as collateral, separately from the premium paid/received. Available cash excludes that collateral. Closing or expiration releases it. Portfolio Greeks are unweighted sums across underlyings and do not imply hedging between different stocks.

Market depth is illustrative synthetic liquidity, not a persistent exchange order book. Single-leg orders fill against generated liquidity with spread/slippage; buying power is checked at execution and cash is not reserved for pending buy orders. Insufficient remaining funds cancel an executable order. Option contracts use a 100-share multiplier and European Black–Scholes pricing with 4% model interest, symbol-level volatility, and no dividends. Expired options and packages cash-settle at intrinsic value in this simulator, unlike physically settled equity options. For packages containing stock, the stock component also liquidates at the expiration mark. The Academy separately illustrates the shares/cash that physical exercise and assignment would produce. Fees, corporate actions, early exercise, borrowing, and margin loans are not modeled. Uncovered calls and short straddles are demonstrated only in the Academy's isolated account.

Chart windows include 1/3/6/12/24 hours and 1 week/1 month/3 months. Retained history includes 1,440 recent observations and up to 370 daily bars. Daily history accumulates as simulation advances. The initial history is synthetic. The displayed market benchmarks are proxies derived from synthetic symbols.

Existing saved accounts migrate in place, preserving cash, holdings, history, and orders. Old close-only historical samples get their open/high/low from successive stored prices; newly generated candles use a simulated intraminute path.

Run engine checks with `node tests/engine.test.js`, `node tests/advanced.test.js`, and `node tests/timeline.test.js`. The timeline tests cover deterministic 90-day replay, exact expiry boundaries, scenario divergence, legacy migration, stock-option strategies, and every mission's example P/L and share-delivery parity. Run end-to-end checks with `node tests/browser.test.js` on macOS with Google Chrome installed. The browser checks use an isolated temporary profile, verify trading, forward/rewind, all 16 mission completions, isolation from the main account, and reload persistence. They write desktop/mobile screenshots under `/tmp` without touching your account.
